## Strict gates

1. **Scientific validity:** pass numerical assumptions, error checks and signed orientation where relevant.
2. **Reproducibility:** pin source commit and verify artifact bytes, not just their supplied hashes.
3. **Authentication and authorization:** verify principal, capability audience/scope/action, expiry, and revocation independently.
4. **Execution semantics:** a `queued` receipt does not imply `completed`; reconcile stable operation ID and request digest before retrying.
5. **Durability:** require persistent idempotency records, signed terminal receipts, and a verified evidence outbox/ledger chain.
6. **Promotion:** exact-head suite and real peer integration tests; human review, no automatic merging or privilege enablement.
